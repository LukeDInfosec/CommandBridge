# Umbraco (ASP.NET CMS on IIS) — content discovery
#
# Umbraco gives itself away with /umbraco (the back office). The findings
# that matter: a reachable installer (/install, /umbraco/install), the
# back-office login, exposed .NET config (web.config, umbracoSettings.config),
# App_Data (which holds the content cache, logs and sometimes the SQLite db
# on older installs), and the diagnostic handlers (elmah.axd, trace.axd).
# The back-office API's timeout endpoint also leaks that Umbraco is present
# and often its behaviour even before auth.
